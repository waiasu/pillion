Reconnect v3 safety fix

- CCU ACTION_ACL_DISCONNECTED immediately enters reconnect wait / STATUS.
- Cancels any pending reconnect attempt on ACL loss.
- While reconnectWait=true, fail() no longer calls stopSelf().
- Notification Stop remains the explicit service shutdown path.
- Reconnect flow remains: ACL_CONNECTED -> 1.5s settle -> rebuild RFCOMM + NaviLite/MirrorEngine only.
- Existing MediaProjection/helper/VD/power behavior is otherwise unchanged.
