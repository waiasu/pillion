Pillion XMAX reconnect v5

Changes from v4:
- Reconnect no longer requires ACTION_ACL_CONNECTED / dashAclConnected=true.
- While reconnectWait=true, probe a brand-new RFCOMM/NaviLite session every 5 seconds.
- ACL_CONNECTED remains a hint and can trigger a quicker 1.5 s probe.
- ACL_DISCONNECTED no longer cancels the pending reconnect loop.
- Failed reconnect attempts always schedule another retry regardless of cached ACL state.
- Reconnect reuses the existing ScreenSource (reuseScreen=true); it does not call screen.start(),
  request MediaProjection again, recreate helper/VD, wake the phone, or force VD promotion.
- Explicit notification Stop remains the real teardown path.

Expected sequence:
  key OFF -> STATUS/reconnectWait -> periodic RFCOMM probes fail harmlessly
  key ON -> either ACL_CONNECTED speeds the next probe or the periodic probe finds YCCU
  -> fresh RFCOMM -> NaviLite handshake -> Streaming -> reconnectWait=false
