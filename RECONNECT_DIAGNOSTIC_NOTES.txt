Pillion XMAX reconnect diagnostic v1

Changes:
- Max FPS range: 1..15 fps (old 0.5 value normalizes to 1)
- Log Bluetooth ACL CONNECTED / DISCONNECTED
- Log RFCOMM EOF (-1), read/write failures, socket/stream state
- Unexpected transport/session loss no longer stops CaptureService
- On loss, demote promoted task and switch to generated STATUS/Waiting frame
- Keep screen source / MediaProjection side alive on unexpected transport failure
- Periodic diagnostic snapshot every 5 seconds
- Notification includes Stop action; Stop performs the real service teardown
- No automatic Bluetooth/RFCOMM reconnect yet
- No wake behavior yet

Useful log filter:
adb logcat -v time Pillion:D *:S
