# Google Material Symbols (CCU linked notification icons)

The CCU linked foreground notification uses Google's **Material Symbols Outlined**, 24px:

- `search` → CCU 圏外
- `search_off` → BT OFF
- `search_check` → CCU 圏内

Source: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/tree/10ecc14790612ca8fd164e3f4ffe9c16fc50d5b7/symbols/web
